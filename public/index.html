How it works

  • Combine two things on the workbench to discover a third. If they don't react it costs nothing — that's how you learn what does.
  • Build puts a machine in your yard that makes that item, over and over, even while you're away.
  • Sell turns what you're holding into coins. Machines can sell their own output automatically.
  • Crafting only reaches as high as your tier. When everything starts coming back "nothing new", buy the next one.
